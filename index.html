Who We Are

Integrated Resource Management for Tanzanian Industry

F&D Resources Ltd is a Tanzania-based company providing integrated waste management, recycling, scrap metal trading, mining and industrial support, logistics, heavy equipment rental and procurement services.

Our head office is located at Roma Street, Kakola, Kahama, Shinyanga, close to major mining and industrial activities. We aim to provide reliable, safety-conscious and cost-efficient services to businesses and public institutions across the region.

Our purpose is simple: turning recoverable materials into economic value while supporting cleaner worksites, efficient industrial operations and sustainable resource use for every client we serve.
