Service Overview

What This Service Covers

Our integrated waste management service covers the full cycle from collection at your site to coordinated delivery at appropriately authorized disposal facilities. We work closely with mining operations, manufacturing plants, construction sites and commercial enterprises across the Shinyanga region.

We seek to reduce avoidable waste, recover usable resources and help clients maintain operational environments that meet applicable environmental and occupational safety standards. Every engagement is planned around your site’s specific waste streams, volumes and access constraints.

Capabilities

What We Can Deliver

A breakdown of the specific capabilities within this service line — each tailored to your site’s operational and compliance needs.

Collection & Transfer

Collection and transfer of general, commercial and non-hazardous industrial waste, with vehicles and crews matched to your site’s access and volume profile.

Sorting & Segregation

On-site sorting and segregation of mixed waste streams to enable recovery of recyclables and proper routing of residuals.

Site Clearance

Industrial site clearance, yard cleaning and sanitation — supporting shutdowns, transitions and ongoing operational hygiene.

Disposal Coordination

Coordination of delivery to appropriately authorized disposal facilities, with documentation aligned to applicable environmental requirements.

Recovery Routing

Identification and routing of recoverable materials — metals, plastics, cardboard, glass and industrial packaging — to recycling pathways.

Cleaning & Sanitation

Yard cleaning and sanitation services that keep operational areas safe, accessible and compliant with site standards.

How It Works

Our Engagement Process

A clear, accountable process from first conversation to ongoing delivery — designed around your operational rhythm.

01

Site Assessment

We review your waste streams, volumes, site layout and access to design an effective collection and segregation plan.

02

Scheduling

Flexible collection schedules are agreed — one-off, periodic or continuous — based on your operational rhythm.

03

On-Site Operations

Trained crews perform collection, sorting and segregation with appropriate PPE and safe material handling.

04

Disposal & Reporting

Waste is delivered to authorized facilities and you receive documentation supporting your compliance records.
